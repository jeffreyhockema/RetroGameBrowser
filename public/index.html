Controllers

Controller Layout 1 puts each button where it was on the console's own controller (A right, B bottom). Right for most Xbox, PlayStation and Switch controllers.

Controller Layout 2 follows the letters on an Xbox controller (A bottom, B right). Try it if a Switch controller's buttons come out swapped in Safari.

For console games. Controllers become players 1 to 4 in the order they're first pressed. A game whose buttons you changed in its own control settings keeps them.
